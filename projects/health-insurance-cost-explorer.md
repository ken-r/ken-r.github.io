## Swiss Public Data

During my civil service at the Federal Office for the Environment, I learnt that Swiss cantons and the federal government release a lot of high-quality open data.
I am also interested in the insurance industry. During my master's, I took the courses *Non-Life Insurance: Mathematics and Statistics*, *Quantitative Risk Management*, and *Machine Learning in Finance and Insurance*.
I brought these interests together by creating the *Health Insurance Cost Explorer* to make health insurance costs easier to explore.

## Data Formats Change

Working with public data also means keeping up with changes. Health insurance premium data is released annually, and it has already been updated once since I started the project. That update caused the pipeline to fail. Many codes changed; for example, `FRAST1` became `FRASTU_01`. Fortunately, the accompanying documentation explained the changes, so it was quite straightforward to adapt the pipeline.
